About

I am a PhD student at Scuola Superiore Meridionale in Naples and a member of Prof. Alioscia Hamma’s Quantum Information research group.

My research focuses on Quantum information theory, with interests in quantum complexity and quantum resource theories. In particular, I study how quantum states behave under quantum maps from the perspective of non-stabilizer resources.

Research interestsQuantum information · Quantum complexity · Quantum resource theories
